About

Philippa Shepherd creates minimalist landscapes and abstract pieces using oil and acrylics. The landscapes are inspired by real places she has visited, spaces from which she seeks to capture the essence rather than a strict reality. The work relies on the accumulation of successive layers and a meticulous approach to texture, allowing the physical paint to take on full meaning. Her technique allows spontaneity in the paintings leading to shapes that can be pulled out of a layer.

The colour palette reflects a duality between serenity and intensity primarily using natural blue, greens and earth tones to anchor the canvases in the living world, while injecting deep, emotional reds to introduce drama and contrast.

Through vigorous and assertive brushwork which aims to breathe drama into otherwise minimalist compositions, delicate and intricate soft brushwork in contrast aims to invite tranquility.

This artistic process is a form of dialogue with the canvas. Rather than following a rigid plan, the paintings are organically built by being guided by what reveals itself before the eyes at each new stage. It is in this precise balance between the power of the gesture, the softness of the atmosphere, and creative intuition that the paintings come to life, inviting the viewer into contemplation.
